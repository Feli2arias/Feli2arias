# Hey, I'm Felipe 👋

I'm from Costa Rica and I like building things fast and seeing if they work. Most of what I make mixes AI, web and crypto, and a lot of it started at a hackathon at 3am.

I study AI Engineering at ULACIT. On the side I run [LaunchLab](https://www.launchlab.lat), a small software agency I founded.

## What I've been building

- **[afterlife](https://github.com/Feli2arias/afterlife)**: a dead man's switch on Solana. If you stop checking in, your crypto goes to your heirs. 🥇 Won the Dev3Pack Global Hackathon with it.
- **[latamstat](https://github.com/Feli2arias/latamstat)**: a dashboard of Latin American indicators with an AI chat that queries the data. 🥈 2nd place at the Databricks x ULACIT hackathon.
- **[settld](https://github.com/Feli2arias/settld)**: split expenses with friends and settle up with real USD₮ transfers.
- **[aicommit](https://github.com/Feli2arias/aicommit)**: a CLI that writes my commit messages, because I'm bad at them.
- **[errordeals-cr](https://github.com/Feli2arias/errordeals-cr)**: hunts for pricing mistakes in Costa Rican online stores.
